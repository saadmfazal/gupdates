AQUANIX PRIVATE-LABEL FOOD PROPOSAL

A specialist range with fewer decisions.

A small range tied to specialist recommendations and repeat food purchases, with minimal extra work for the fish room.

What we propose to supply: packaged dry fish food under your brand, with agreed species scope, formula, particle format and quality specification. Manufacturing, labeling, packaging and delivery arrangements require technical and commercial confirmation before an order.

Initial range: Begin with two candidate feeding roles: Mbuna Green and Tanganyika Daily. Dave would approve the species scope and feeding guidance. Predator Condition is a later option, not a required launch SKU.

Packaging direction: 100 g discovery packs and 250 g regular-use packs; final sizes depend on filling tests and demand.

First decision: choose one priority feeding role and a technical reviewer. Then assess a documented sample and agree an initial order only after delivered cost, minimum batches, shelf life and sell-through have been reviewed.

This sequence is illustrative. Each stage depends on approval, testing and production availability. No launch date, price, supply capability or performance result is promised here.
