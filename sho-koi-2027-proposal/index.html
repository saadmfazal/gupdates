WHY NOT PRINT EVENLY?Bag usage is not equal.

Frank's order shows the two Signature 5 lb. core formulas moving almost twice the Advantage Pro 5 lb. lines and roughly three times Color Intensity. The 19 lb. bags use far fewer pieces because each bag carries much more food. The MOQ strategy therefore follows actual bag consumption.

March onward · call-off model

The 10th is the dispatch date.
Not the order date.

Once the opening inventory is built, we stop guessing. Frank gives us forward demand, we hold the inputs, and each production run is made against the next real shipment.

Commercial structure

Separate the first packaging investment.
Keep the food price clean.

The first order was deliberately compressed to prove the product and win the business. The food quality held; the packaging system did not. The 2027 model fixes that without hiding a large first-run print investment inside the food rate.

Historical first order $1.22 / lb.

The previous full-order rate was built around bulk purchasing, low-cost packaging and shipping consolidation. At the time, a standard-cost approach was closer to approximately $1.84/lb.
