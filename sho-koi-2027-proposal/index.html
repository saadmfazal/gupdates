Packaging investment

Separate the first bag investment from the food.

The first professional print run carries setup costs and MOQs that belong to the 2027 packaging program, not to one shipment of food. Keeping it separate makes the economics cleaner and prevents the food price from being distorted by the first print setup.

Why this is cleaner

01
Food remains the food price.

Product pricing covers manufacturing and delivered logistics into New York, without hiding the first packaging setup inside the kilo rate.

02
The first print is a 2027 asset.

Cylinders, plates, printing setup and unused bags support multiple orders through the year.

03
MOQs follow actual demand.

Fast sellers receive 5,000-piece runs; mid movers 3,000; slow movers only 1,000.

04
Future pricing becomes simpler.

After the first packaging program is established, normal bag cost can be normalized into later product pricing.
